Make reordering one click. **Buy again** shows each customer the products they buy most, and puts
a **Buy this again** button on every line of an order. It suits shops with consumables and
repeat purchases — tea, cosmetics, pet food, refills.

## Where it appears
- **Orders** page: a grid of the customer's most-purchased products, each with a **Buy again**
  button. Add the **Optimalify Buy Again** block where you want the grid in the editor.
- **Order status** page: a **Buy this again** button on each order line.

Customers with no past orders see nothing.

## Set it up
1. **Apps → Optimalify → Blocks → Buy again**.
2. Adjust the settings (below), set **Status = Active**, and **Save**.
3. Add the **Optimalify Buy Again** block to the Orders page in the customer account editor — see
   [Add blocks to your pages](add-blocks-in-editor.md).

## Settings

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Grid title** | Shown above the grid. Leave blank to use the built-in title in each customer's language. |
| **Products to show** | 1 to 12 of the products the customer buys most. |
| **After clicking** | **Open the cart** or **Go straight to checkout**. |

## How it works
Customer accounts can't add items to a cart, so each button opens your storefront with the
product already in the cart. Line item properties (such as engraving text) aren't carried over,
and products that are deleted or sold out are hidden.
