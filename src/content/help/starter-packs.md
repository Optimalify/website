Not sure where to start? A starter pack adds the blocks for a goal in one click, already filled
in with starter text you can edit.

## The packs
Open **Apps → Optimalify → Dashboard** and pick one:

| Pack | Adds |
|---|---|
| **Fewer support tickets** | [FAQ](faq.md) + [Support](support.md) + [Social links](social-links.md) |
| **EU & B2B ready** | [Invoice download](invoice.md) + [Profile](profile.md) (Company name and VAT number fields) + a [banner](banner.md) for business buyers |
| **Promote & retain** | [Rich content](rich-content.md) + [Banner](banner.md) + [Trust badges](trust-badges.md) |

## What happens when you click
1. The pack adds its blocks, filled in with starter text.
2. Blocks you've **already set up are never changed**.
3. On the **Free** plan, only the **first new block is switched on**; the rest are saved
   **switched off** (Free runs one active block at a time). **Pro** unlocks all blocks.
4. Click **Place blocks in the editor** — it opens the checkout & accounts editor on the
   Optimalify blocks, where you add them to your pages. See
   [Add blocks to your pages](add-blocks-in-editor.md).

## Finish the blocks that need your input
Some blocks don't show until you add details:

- **Social links** — add your links.
- **Invoice** — add your business address and tax ID.
- **Support** — add a support email, if your store has none.

Edit the starter text of any block to match your brand, then **Save**.

Need a hand? Use **Chat with us** in the app to talk to the developer live.
