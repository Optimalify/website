Customers see the account pages in the language they shop in. Optimalify shows each block's
text in that language when you've translated it, and your original text when you haven't.

## What gets translated
- **Built-in wording** — buttons and default labels such as "Download invoice" — comes
  translated in English, Vietnamese, German, French, Spanish, Dutch and Italian. Nothing to set up.
- **Your own text** in Banner, Support, Social links, FAQ, Store policies, Rich content, Trust
  badges, Payment icons and Custom pages: you translate it, in either of the two ways below.
- **Profile custom fields** have their own per-language labels in the Profile block.

## Before you start
Add the languages you sell in under **Settings → Languages** in your Shopify admin and publish
them. Only published languages appear in Optimalify.

## Option 1: translate inside Optimalify
1. **Apps → Optimalify → Translations**.
2. Pick a **language**, then a **block**.
3. Type each translation next to the original text and **Save**.

Customers see the new text right away. A field you leave empty shows your original text.

## Option 2: translate in Translate & Adapt
Every block's text is also available in Shopify's **Translate & Adapt** app, under the
Optimalify entries (one entry per block text, FAQ question, policy, badge or link). Translate
there as you would any other content.

Translate & Adapt changes reach customers the next time Optimalify syncs — automatically each
time you open the Optimalify app, or straight away with **Translations → Sync translations**.

## Good to know
- When you change the original text, Shopify marks its translations **outdated**. Outdated
  translations are not shown — customers see the new original until you update or confirm the
  translation ("Still correct" in Optimalify).
- Regional languages fall back to the base language: a customer browsing in French (Canada)
  sees your French translation if there is no French (Canada) one.
- Translations are included on the Free plan.
