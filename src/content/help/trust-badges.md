Reassure customers with trust badges — secure checkout, money-back guarantee, fast shipping,
and more — on the **Order status** page, right after the fulfillment details.

## Where it appears
Order status page, after the fulfillment details section. Add the **Optimalify Badges** block
in the editor.

## Set it up
1. **Apps → Optimalify → Blocks → Badges**.
2. Add a heading/message, add your badges (below), set **Status = Active**, and **Save**.
3. Add the **Optimalify Badges** block in the customer account editor.

## Content
- **Heading** (max 80) and **Message** (max 300) shown above the badges.

## Badges (up to 8)
Add **preset** badges with one click, or build **custom** ones. Every badge uses the same
editor row, so presets are fully editable too.

**Presets:** Secure checkout · Money-back guarantee · Fast shipping · Authentic products.

**Each badge has:**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The badge label (max 40). |
| **Description** | A short line under the title, shown in the Cards style (max 80). |
| **Icon** | Pick from the curated set (Lock, Check circle, Check, Credit card, Money back, Discount, Shipping, Returns, Star, Gift card, Worldwide, Store, Fast) — or **None**. |
| **Tone** | Icon colour: `auto`, `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, `critical`. |
| **Logo** | Optional. Pick an image from your store's **Files** (Settings → Files). A logo **replaces** the icon. |

Use **Move up / Move down** to reorder, the toggle to **show/hide** a badge, and **Remove**
to delete one.

> **Logos come from your store's Files only** — you choose from images already uploaded to
> Settings → Files. Nothing is uploaded from your computer here, so customers only ever see
> images hosted in your store.

## Display

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Badge style** | **Cards** (icon + title + description) or **Compact pills** (chips). |
| **Layout** | **Inline row**, **Two columns**, or **Stacked**. |
| **Alignment** | **Left** or **Center**. |

## Tips
- **Cards** style is best when you use descriptions; **Compact pills** when you want a light,
  single-line strip.
- Add a brand **logo** to a preset (e.g. your carrier's logo on "Fast shipping").
- Keep it to 3–4 strong badges — more isn't always better.
